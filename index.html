About

Biography

Prof. P. Ramkumar joined the Department of Mechanical Engineering at IIT Madras as an Assistant Professor in 2012, was promoted to Associate Professor in 2019, and has been a full Professor since January 2024. Before joining IIT Madras, he was an Associate Professor at SSN College of Engineering, Chennai. He completed his B.E. in Mechanical Engineering at the College of Engineering, Guindy, Anna University, followed by an M.Tech. at IIT Madras. He went on to earn a Ph.D. from the University of Southampton, UK, and completed postdoctoral research at the University of Leicester, UK.

His research centres on tribology — the study of friction, wear, and lubrication — with a focus on developing new materials and coatings for engine components, nano-lubrication, gearbox design, brake materials, and condition-monitoring techniques for rotating machinery.

He leads the Advanced Tribology Research Lab (ATRL), with research spanning automotive tribology, tribo-design of machine components, biotribology, wind turbine gearbox bearing failures (white etching cracks and hydrogen embrittlement), surface engineering (textures and coatings for bio-implants), and FEM-based wear prediction.

He has authored over 55 peer-reviewed journal papers, holds three patents, and has led sponsored research projects worth over ₹19 crore (approx. US$2.3 million) funded by DST-SERB, the Ministry of Education's Institution of Eminence scheme, and industry partners including Caterpillar, ZF, GE, Kone Elevators, and Delphi-TVS.
